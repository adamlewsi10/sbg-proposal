The Client

A compact marketing team serves 30,000+ advisers weekly with regulatory, commercial, and event communications across all four brands.

The Challenges

Where things stand today

From previous conversations, here is what we heard.

01

Email Takes 3x Longer

Building and sending an email campaign in D365 Customer Insights takes approximately three times longer than the previous platform. The team is roughly 40% behind in output capacity.

300% slower email production
02

Reporting Is Broken

Email analytics don’t match journey analytics, leaving the team unsure which numbers to trust. A single quarterly report extraction took 3.5 weeks to complete manually.

3.5 weeks for one report
03

Segmentation Bottleneck

Creating a targeted list requires requesting fields from the data team, cross-referencing with IT for validation, and waiting days before a segment is ready. No self-service capability.

Days to build a single segment
04

Events Platform Gap

The D365 events portal was trialled and abandoned as not fit for purpose. The team reverted to CVent, which is effective but carries significant licensing cost across 130+ events per year.

130+ events, high CVent cost
05

No Marketing Attribution

No way to demonstrate marketing ROI. No AB testing in practice, no campaign measurement, no omnichannel reporting. Marketing is perceived as a communications function, not a revenue driver.

Zero attribution capability
06

Multi-Brand Complexity

Four brands, each with their own domains and consent requirements. Managing separate preference centres, transactional emails for regulatory content, and brand-specific content is extremely difficult in the current platform.

4 brands, 1 team

The Approach

Two Phases, Two Workstreams, One Platform

Phase 1 delivers an MVP — your team sending email marketing from HubSpot. Phase 2 rolls out the full marketing engine. Both workstreams run in parallel within each phase.

Phase 1

MVP — Email Marketing Live

Milestone: First campaign sent from HubSpot

Data & Platform

  • HubSpot portal setup and Business Unit configuration (x4 brands)
  • Data model design — properties, associations, lifecycle stages
  • Contact data migration from D365 (cleansing, deduplication, mapping)
  • Multi-brand consent and preference centre architecture
  • D365 bi-directional integration (native sync or middleware)
  • User provisioning, permissions, and security

Marketing Activation

  • Brand email templates designed and built per brand
  • Core segmentation lists (weekly newsletters, regulatory, partner comms)
  • Transactional email setup for regulatory “must read” comms
  • Initial automation workflows (welcome, event registration)
  • Marketing team training and onboarding
  • First campaign: plan, build, send, report
Phase 2

Full Rollout — Marketing Engine at Scale

Milestone: Full omnichannel marketing + events

Data & Platform

  • OneView API integration scoping and build
  • Data governance framework and naming conventions
  • Advanced reporting datasets and custom properties
  • Lead scoring and engagement scoring models
  • Integration monitoring and error handling

Marketing Activation

  • Event management rollout + BBD Boom event module deployment
  • Social media publishing, inbox, and ads integration
  • WhatsApp channel activation
  • Campaign and attribution reporting dashboards
  • AB testing framework and smart content
  • AI brand voice setup per brand
  • Advanced automation (nurture sequences, event lifecycle, partner comms)
